EL DORADO

AREA

El Dorado is situated with south east exposure on the north end of Three Mile Lake. With minimal boat traffic in the surrounding bay, El Dorado offers a very calming and serene atmosphere.

PROPERTY

EL DORADO - Perched atop a beautiful Muskoka landscape, amongst the trees, El Dorado offers panoramic views of the surrounding bay. As you approach the cottage, you will pass by a brand new private sports court that offers up pickle ball and basketball. This beautiful property has 280ft of frontage which provides renters with both comfort and privacy. The open-concept main floor walks out onto a very spacious and sunlit deck, bordered by a glass railing and furnished with lounge seating. A naturally designed stone stairway lowers you down towards the waterfront. Halfway down the pathway sits a fire pit surrounded by 6 Muskoka chairs - perfect for roasts and evening relaxation. The dock features a Sea-Doo slip and deep water off the end, plus a floating swimming platform about 25 yards off the dock.

INTERIOR

MAIN COTTAGE — MAIN LEVEL
KITCHEN

Open-concept kitchen boasting high-end stainless steel appliances, double-sided fridge, and a large island with seating.

DINING ROOM

Open-concept dining room with a custom wooden dining table for 10, amazing views and a walkout to the deck. A wood-burning stove separates the kitchen and living room.

LIVING ROOM

The living room has vaulted ceilings, abundant windows with lake views, comfortable seating and a gas fireplace.

LOWER LEVEL — REC ROOM

Spacious rec room with relaxation seating, a flat screen TV, and a games area with bar/kitchenette, beverage fridge, dishwasher, darts board and ping-pong/pool table.

INCLUSIONS / EXCLUSIONS

This cottage is equipped with air conditioning and a manual generator. All properties are outfitted with Jayne's 5-Star quality white linens, BBQ, unlimited internet/WiFi, Smart TVs, laundry facilities, dishwasher, drip coffee maker, vacuum and cleaning supplies. The cottage includes midweek housekeeping during peak season rentals. Boathouses, bunkies and 3-season Muskoka rooms are not habitable from Thanksgiving to Victoria Day. Outdoor furniture and water toys are unavailable during that period for safety reasons.
