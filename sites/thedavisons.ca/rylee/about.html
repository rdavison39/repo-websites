How it started

I started playing hockey at six, moving through house league and rep hockey. At the same time, I trained in gymnastics and tumbling. In 2010, I made the Canadian national tumbling team and competed at the world level. Eventually I chose to focus on hockey.

What I love about hockey

I love battling for the puck, skating hard, scoring as a team and the friendships that continue long after playing together. Hockey has taught me the value of commitment, teamwork and competing hard.
