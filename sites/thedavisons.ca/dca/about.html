About Sydney

Hi, peeps. Don't cringe—but I love y'all. It's a “S” for Sydney. It goes “SID.” Let's do this, let's roll… preferably in the right direction.

This site celebrates the special talent of getting turned around and the ability to laugh about it afterwards.

The DCA philosophy

We believe that being directionally challenged is not a character flaw. It is an opportunity for unexpected detours, memorable stories, and discovering places you never meant to visit.

Our guiding principles

Stay calm

Recalculating is a lifestyle, not a failure.

Ask for help

Even if you need to ask twice. Or three times.

Enjoy the detour

You might not know where you are, but at least you have a story.
