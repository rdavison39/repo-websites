Is DCA for you?

Some of us tried every map, followed every instruction, and still arrived somewhere unexpected. We decided to face our condition with honesty, humour, and the occasional emergency phone call.

Unofficial diagnostic: If you answer “yes” to four or more questions below, you may be directionally challenged. This is a joke, not a medical diagnosis. Your mileage—and route—may vary.

The six-question navigation challenge

1. The “How did you get lost?” moment

When someone asks how you managed to get lost, does your explanation require a map, a timeline, and a witness?

2. GPS said what?

Have you been given the exact address, detailed directions, and a GPS—yet the route still looks like modern art?

3. Dress for asking directions

Do you make sure you look your best before heading out, just in case asking a stranger for directions becomes the main event?

4. A GPS for every occasion

Do you use several navigation devices and still struggle with a supposedly simple pickup?

5. Choose your own adventure

When someone asks you to pick something up, do the words “left,” “right,” and “nearby” suddenly lose all meaning?

6. Where is home again?

When someone asks if you know the way home from here, does your face say “I was hoping you did”?
