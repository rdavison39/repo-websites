Original description

This 1990 Case 580K 4WD was purchased for property work including clearing land, trenching for power and building a septic bed. Once that work was complete, the owner began looking for a new home for the machine.

The owner described investing in maintenance and repairs over several years, partly as a hobby. The machine was reported to start reliably even after sitting idle through the winter. It originally came from South Carolina and was described as having relatively little cab rust for its age.

Service and parts noted

  • New NAPA wet heavy-equipment battery
  • New starter
  • Newer rear tire
  • Driver-side door refurbished
  • Dipper seal replaced
  • New hydraulic hose for bucket return
  • Hydraulic pressure gauge installed
  • New seat base
  • New right-hand-side window
  • Main boom cylinder honed and rebuilt
  • Fluids serviced and various seals replaced
  • Passenger window and rear window panels replaced

Market context

The original page described the Case 580K as competing in the 14-foot backhoe and 50–70 horsepower market, alongside models from Caterpillar, Deere, Ford, JCB, Massey and CDS.
