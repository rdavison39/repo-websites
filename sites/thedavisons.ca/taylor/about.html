My background

I started playing hockey at age six, moving from house league into rep hockey. I was also heavily involved in gymnastics and trained intensively. In 2009, I focused on gymnastics and competed nationally, placing fourth overall at the Canadian National Championships in Prince Edward Island.

In 2011, I returned to competitive girls hockey and never looked back. I love skating hard, battling for the puck, celebrating team goals and—above all—the friendships that come from playing together.
